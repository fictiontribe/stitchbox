## SILHOUETTE & FACELESS STUDIES

Portrait silhouette against stark white cyclorama, subject facing camera head-on. Face rendered in complete graphic black shadow while the background stays pure, clean white. Only the crown outline and skull structure suggested as a hard-edged geometric shape against the bright field. Eyes, nose, mouth entirely absent — a flat black graphic void. Neck and shoulders read as a sharp-edged silhouette cut cleanly against the white. Shot on Hasselblad, 80mm f/2.8, f/5.6. Sharp digital clarity, deep true blacks, clean whites, graphic high-contrast lighting. Conceptual portrait, faceless anonymity study, 032C graphic minimalism.

---

Macro close-up portrait from shoulders up, subject facing directly forward, head tilted slightly upward. Face fully obscured in flat black shadow — eyes, nose, mouth completely invisible. Only visible: a razor-sharp rim light illuminating both sides of the face (camera-left and camera-right), tracing a clean graphic edge along cheekbones, jawline, and temples. Bare shoulders and upper chest, smooth skin rendered in hyper-detailed clinical texture. Pure black seamless backdrop, zero separation between face shadow and background. Dual hard rim lights from left and right, sculptural chiaroscuro. Shot on Canon EOS 5D Mark III, 85mm f/1.2L, f/2. Extreme high contrast, deep true blacks, sculptural shadow play, hyper-detailed texture rendering. Fine art portrait, faceless anonymity with sculptural body presence, SCHÖN cover energy.

---

Medium close-up portrait, subject positioned so the face is completely backlit and thrown into flat graphic shadow. Crown barely legible as a hard-edged silhouette; no facial features distinguishable. Pure black negative space surrounds the shape. Severe direct backlight creates a razor-thin rim outline and nothing else. Subject reads as entirely unidentifiable, more graphic mark than portrait. Shot on Hasselblad, 80mm f/2.8, f/4. Sharp digital clarity, deep true blacks, clean whites, no grain or color cast. Fine art portrait, anonymity, faceless study, clinical editorial precision.

---

Close portrait, subject facing directly toward camera. Face rendered as a solid black graphic shape — no features visible whatsoever. Only the top edge of the skull reads as a crisp silhouette line against a stark white backdrop. Neck and shoulders merge into the same flat black shape, creating one continuous graphic silhouette. Single hard light source from directly behind subject, producing a thin, precise rim line along the hairline only. Shot on Canon EOS 5D Mark III, 50mm f/1.2L, f/1.8. Sharp digital clarity, deep true blacks, clean whites, sculptural shadow play. Anonymity study, brutalist graphic portrait.

---

Figure emerging from a stark black void — a graphic, featureless silhouette. Medium shot, human figure identifiable only by the hard-edged outline of skull, neck, and shoulders. Face reads as a flat black shape with zero internal detail. Pure black background eliminates any depth cue. Minimal rim light traces only the very top of the head in a thin, precise line. Shot on Hasselblad, 50mm f/3.5, f/4. Sharp digital clarity, deep true blacks, graphic high-contrast lighting, clinical editorial precision. Conceptual, loss-of-identity study, 032C brutalist minimalism.

---

Faceless shadow study — identity fully obscured through graphic contrast rather than blur. Close-up, subject's head positioned so the entire face reads as a flat, crisp-edged black shape. No identifiable features. Dark neutral seamless background providing zero separation. A single hard backlight creates one precise rim line along the hair only. Shot on Canon EOS 5D Mark III, 85mm f/1.2L, f/2.2. Sharp digital clarity, deep true blacks, sculptural shadow play, clinical editorial precision. Fine art gallery aesthetic, anonymity and graphic form.
